About

Sheila Cohen Furr
Ph.D., A.B.N.

Board Certified Psychologist and Accredited Collaborative Professional, experienced in helping families navigate stress, change, and complex family dynamics before, during, and after divorce.

Dr. Furr works with children, adolescents, and adults to support healthy coping, emotional security, and more fulfilling relationships. She serves families in the collaborative and mediation process as a Family Mediator, Child Specialist, and Neutral Facilitator, helping reduce conflict and keep the focus on the family’s well-being and future.

Dr. Furr is the founding president of the South Palm Beach County Collaborative Law Group and serves on the boards of Ruth and Norman Rales Jewish Family Service and the Florida Academy of Collaborative Professionals. She is a member of the medical staff of Boca Raton Regional Hospital.
