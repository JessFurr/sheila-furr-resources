How I Help

Support at every stage

My practice focuses on helping children, adolescents, and adults develop healthy coping strategies to feel safe and secure in themselves and to build more fulfilling interpersonal relationships. Whether you are weighing a decision, in the middle of a life challenge, or rebuilding afterward, there is a path forward grounded in respect and cooperation.

01

Before Divorce

Consultation for couples deciding about divorce, parenting plans, life planning, and how to tell the children — in a safe, non-adversarial setting.

02

During Divorce

Collaborative facilitation, child specialist services, mediation, and consultation to address the emotional aspects of divorce peacefully.

03

After Divorce

Co-parenting counseling, therapy for children and adults of divorce, mediation, and family therapy to help everyone move forward.
